  • Yogurt with Granola

    Breakfast

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup yogurt
    • 1/4 cup granola
    • 1/4 cup berries

    Instructions

    1. Add yogurt to a bowl.
    2. Top with granola and berries

  • BLT Sandwich

    Lunch

    Ingredients

    • 2 slices bread
    • 2 slices cooked bacon
    • 2 lettuce leaves
    • 2 tomato slices
    • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise

    Instructions

    1. Toast the bread and spread with mayonnaise.
    2. Add cooked bacon, lettuce, and tomato
    3. Top with the second slice of bread.

  • Avocado Toast

    Breakfast or lunch

    Ingredients

    • 1 slice bread
    • 1/2 Avocado
    • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
    • Salt and pepper to taste

    Instructions

    1. Toast the bread.
    2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice.
    3. Spread on toast and season.

  • Kimchi Fried Rice

    Dinner

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup cooked rice
    • 1/2 cup chopped kimchi
    • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
    • 1 teaspoon soy sauce

    Instructions

    1. Heat the oil in a pan.
    2. Add kimchi and cook for 2 minutes
    3. Add rice and soy sauce.
    4. Stir until thoroughly heated.
